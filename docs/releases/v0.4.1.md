Vessel 0.4.1 shows where a closed Play's stops started, and makes the chart's
active timeframe easier to see.

This release has no migrations and no configuration changes.

## Highlights

- **Original and final stops of a closed Play.** When you moved a stop after
  planning, for example to break-even once the entry filled, a Closed Play now
  shows where it started. The chart draws each original stop the entry no longer
  has as a faint, fixed `SL orig` line (`E2 SL1 orig` with several entries or
  stops), beside the stop the Play closed with. The Review tab's "Stops moved"
  section lists each moved entry, how many times it moved, its original stops
  with their plan revision, and its final stops.
- **Read from what is already saved.** The comparison uses the Play's existing
  plan revisions. A percentage stop is resolved at that revision's entry price
  and leverage, so restating the same price in another unit is not a move. Stop
  prices are shown exactly as saved. Nothing shows when no stop moved or the
  history cannot be read.
- **Clearer active timeframe.** The selected timeframe on the chart is a filled,
  outlined, bold chip instead of a text colour change only. Its text stays
  readable in all ten built-in themes.

## Upgrading from 0.4.0

1. Replace the deploy files with this release's versions and set
   `VESSEL_VERSION=0.4.1`.
2. Pull matching API and web images. There are no new identity or journal
   migrations; running the explicit `migrate` service finds nothing to apply.
3. Restart the services. Upgrade API and web together.

Follow the [start sequence](https://github.com/sloppitty/vessel/blob/v0.4.1/deploy/README.md#start-and-connect).
Installations older than 0.4.0 must first follow the
[0.4.0 upgrade](https://github.com/sloppitty/vessel/blob/v0.4.1/docs/releases/v0.4.0.md#upgrading-from-035),
including its thirteen journal migrations and the one-time
`play-search-rebuild --apply`. Rolling back to 0.4.0 needs only the previous
images and deploy files, since no schema changed.

## Known limits

- Original stops are shown for Closed Plays only, not Cancelled, Unresolved or
  retrospective ones. They never change initial R, the scored result, Review or
  sizing.
- Chart drawings, including Shift-drag measurements, are still not kept on
  Closed, Cancelled or Unresolved Plays, which keep their plan.
- Wallet acceptance §10 remains **not signed off**: the real-wallet/non-mainnet
  EOA check is owner-pending. Keep registration `Closed` until it is recorded in
  [the identity state](https://github.com/sloppitty/vessel/blob/v0.4.1/docs/architecture/multi-user-state.md).
