Vessel 0.4.0 adds risk and coverage tools around your Plays, a journal-wide
search, Hyperliquid builder markets and one instrument selector for every venue.

This release requires thirteen journal migrations and a one-time search index
step. There is no new identity migration and there are no required configuration
settings.

## Highlights

- **Open risk.** The loss if every current stop fills across your Planned and
  Open Plays, with long and short shown apart and never netted. A stop at or
  beyond its entry adds nothing, and a Play that cannot be measured is counted as
  unknown, never partly summed. Overview has an Open risk tile and the Plays
  capital context shows the Play's own increment as you edit it.
- **Soft limits, never enforced.** Settings → Risk limits holds an optional open
  risk limit and daily and weekly loss limits in R. All start blank. A reached
  limit is flagged in Suggestions and at Plan it, where you can add a cooldown
  note. Nothing is blocked.
- **Weekly review.** Review's Week view shows one reporting week: results,
  exclusions, process cases, repeated mistake tags, your latest next actions and
  the Plays waiting for review. Settings → Reporting week sets its time zone and
  first day (UTC and Monday until you change them).
- **If-then rule.** One rule per trading mode, written from the Thesis tab. Plan
  it asks for an acknowledgement or an exception with a reason, never blocking,
  the review asks whether you followed it, and Review counts the answers.
- **Search the journal.** Titles, theses, invalidations, strategies, evidence
  notes, reviews, log entries and image notes, with the matching field and an
  excerpt. Ended Plays in the list show their exit, result and estimated R.
- **Keep execution history, and backfill gaps.** Accounts can keep their
  execution history (Manage account → Keep execution history); the monitor reads
  it on its schedule, behind protection checks. Hyperliquid gaps can be
  backfilled from Account detail, and only the part Vessel can prove is closed.
  Backfilled executions never raise notifications.
- **Better protection warnings.** An open position with no resting stop and no
  Open Play is flagged on accounts that keep history. A stop beyond the venue's
  liquidation price is critical. "Link this stop" attaches a compatible resting
  stop order, and the undersized-stop, missing-stop and position-size warnings
  also open when the position or order keeps changing size.
- **Omissions are visible.** Executions an import leaves out (Lighter
  liquidations, deleverages, settlements, fee-bearing trades, rows without
  reported P&L, unrecognized markets) leave durable markers. Activity no longer
  invents a phantom opposite operation across them, and an order with an omitted
  execution is never settled or scored.
- **Split exits and flips.** Several Plays closed by one exit fill, or a flip fill
  that closes one Play and opens another, can be split by you, conserving the
  fill's quantity and splitting its fee pro rata. Each such Play closes with its
  own local result; the venue result reads "Not allocated" and stays out of
  Review's scores and sizing.
- **Funding.** The monitor worker reads settled funding about every 6 hours
  (`Vessel:Funding:Interval`) for accounts with an Open Play or kept history, after
  protection checks. History fills in window by window where a venue's page budget
  cannot hold 30 days. A Play shows its funding only when it is provably the only
  position on its market over its holding window. Funding is never added to a
  result, and missing funding is never shown as zero.
- **Exports.** A saved Play downloads as a zip (plan revisions, history,
  executions, review, log and evidence notes as JSON, evidence originals
  unchanged). Settings → Data export gives an account's executions or funding as
  CSV, and Review can download its current selection as CSV. Amounts stay exact,
  text cells are protected against spreadsheet formulas, and none of it is a tax
  report.
- **Cost assumptions.** Settings → Cost assumptions holds your round-trip fee and
  slippage per venue. A Play's summary shows the assumed cost beside its planned
  figures. Nothing else reads it.
- **Placed before this Play was saved.** The Execution tab lists compatible orders
  placed up to 24 hours before you saved the Play, so a fast entry you journaled
  afterwards can be linked by hand. Vessel never links them by itself.
- **Hyperliquid builder markets.** Builder markets such as gold (`xyz:GOLD`),
  stocks and indices can be planned, charted and tracked with their positions,
  orders, fills and funding. USDC-margined builder DEXes only; balances stay the
  primary DEX's. Settings → Market sources hides a source from the selector
  without affecting Plays on its markets.
- **One instrument selector.** The same selector serves every venue, with Category,
  Source and Instrument columns when a venue has them, and search by the venue's
  keywords (`xau` finds gold).
- **Fixes.** Hyperliquid chart and suggestion prices follow the venue's decimal
  limit, and a wallet read failure no longer blocks the perpetuals refresh.
  RISEx history pages share one fixed end time. When a venue later reports a
  retained fill with different values, Vessel keeps the first copy and lists the
  differences in Account detail. See the
  [changelog](https://github.com/sloppitty/vessel/blob/v0.4.0/CHANGELOG.md) for
  the full list.

## Upgrading from 0.3.5

1. Stop web, every API and every worker, including `worker-notifications` and
   any optional Paper worker. Back up both identity and journal schemas,
   evidence and the credential key ring. Keep your Compose project, owner GUID
   and overlays.
2. Replace the deploy files with this release's versions and set
   `VESSEL_VERSION=0.4.0`.
3. Pull matching API and web images and run the explicit `migrate` service. It
   applies, in order, `20261021144248_RiskSettings`,
   `20261021164349_RiskContext`, `20261021180627_FillImportConflicts`,
   `20261022100811_ReportingSettings`, `20261022104353_LossLimits`,
   `20261022124043_IfThenRule`, `20261022143200_HistoryRetention`,
   `20261022182854_HistoryBackfill`, `20261022185700_ExecutionOmissions`,
   `20261022193325_FundingSchedule`, `20261023075337_JournalSearch`,
   `20261023083202_FillAllocations` and `20261102123723_MarketSources`. No
   application host migrates at startup.
4. Run `play-search-rebuild --apply` once so existing Plays are searchable (it
   dry-runs by default; see the
   [journal search index](https://github.com/sloppitty/vessel/blob/v0.4.0/deploy/README.md#journal-search-index)).
   Until then search counts them as "not indexed yet".
5. Start the services. Upgrade API and web together.

Follow the [start sequence](https://github.com/sloppitty/vessel/blob/v0.4.0/deploy/README.md#start-and-connect).
Earlier installations must also follow their intervening upgrade requirements.
Rollback requires the pre-upgrade backup, previous deploy files and private
configuration with matching previous images. The down migrations of
`FillAllocations` and `UnresolvedStatus` refuse to run once allocations or
Unresolved history exist, and a rolling downgrade is not supported. Follow the
deployment guide's
[restore procedure](https://github.com/sloppitty/vessel/blob/v0.4.0/deploy/README.md#isolated-restore),
including identity reset and event-epoch rotation before restarting hosts.

## Known limits

- Open risk, limits, the week page and cost assumptions are estimates and flags.
  Vessel enforces nothing and is not advice. External alert channels for
  critical events are not part of this release; the monitor checks periodically,
  not continuously, so it is not stop protection.
- Allocated shared exits and flips keep the venue result "Not allocated", out of
  Review's scores and sizing. Funding is shown beside results and never added to
  them.
- Backfill covers Hyperliquid only. What the venue no longer serves is marked
  beyond its reach. Lighter liquidations, deleverages, settlements and
  fee-bearing trades are still left out of history and scoring, now with markers.
- Hyperliquid builder markets cover USDC-margined DEXes only, and balances stay
  the primary DEX's. Turning a source off only filters the selector.
- Real-phone pairing on an HTTPS origin and the physical camera scanner still need
  an owner check (see
  [the pairing state](https://github.com/sloppitty/vessel/blob/v0.4.0/docs/architecture/device-pairing-state.md)).
- Wallet acceptance §10 remains **not signed off**: the real-wallet/non-mainnet EOA
  check is owner-pending. Keep registration `Closed` until it is recorded in
  [the identity state](https://github.com/sloppitty/vessel/blob/v0.4.0/docs/architecture/multi-user-state.md).
