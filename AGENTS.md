October 15, 2026, before the monitor stack (#43–#47) merged: the owner replaced its delivery design and accepted the review corrections; follow the October 15 entries in `docs/architecture/play-monitor-plan.md`. Delivery uses Azure Service Bus–style topics and subscriptions on RabbitMQ through Wolverine: publishers append the event and outbox envelope in the unit of work and publish once to the `vessel.events` topic (routing key = event type), knowing nothing about subscribers. Each subscription is a module-declared quorum queue `vessel.events.sub.<name>` with immutable filter bindings, a transport delivery limit and its own `.dlq`, provisioned only by the explicit, insert-only provision step, with its registration, low-water mark and receipts in PostgreSQL. Listeners run inline (no durable inbox; the outbox stays) and one dispatcher settles live and catch-up deliveries through the receipt, which is the business retry limit; DLQs are diagnostic only. Recovery is the subscriber's own catch-up from the database, never a producer republish. Retention never extends past `Vessel:Events:RetentionDays`: only pending receipts hold the floor, and a subscription that falls behind becomes lagging with a visible gap (deregister removed subscriptions so their pending receipts stop holding it). Commands use module-owned queues such as `vessel.commands.monitor`; PostgreSQL stays the record. The browser stream stays a database-cursor reader with a stream epoch; the restore procedure must run `rotate-event-epoch` before any host starts.
